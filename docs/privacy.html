Home Eats — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Home Eats is a personal meal-planning app for recipes, weekly plans, and grocery lists. This page explains what happens to your data when you use it.

The short version

Home Eats does not have user accounts, does not run analytics or advertising, and does not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. Your recipes, meal plans, and grocery lists are stored only on your own device.

What's stored, and where

  • On your device only: your recipes, meal plans, grocery lists, saved restaurants, and any household member/vote information you add. None of this is uploaded to or stored on any Home Eats server. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Camera

If you grant camera access, it's used only to photograph a recipe (a cookbook page, recipe card, or handwritten note) so it can be turned into a recipe in your recipe box. The photo is processed to extract the recipe text (see "AI recipe extraction" below) and is not stored on any Home Eats server afterward.

Location

If you grant location access ("While Using the App"), your approximate location is used only to show nearby restaurants first when you search, and is sent only as part of that search request. It is not stored, logged, or used for any other purpose.

AI recipe extraction and recommendations

When you import a recipe from a photo or notes, or ask for recipe recommendations, the photo and/or text you provide is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for processing, through Home Eats' own backend server. This data is used only to generate the recipe or recommendation returned to the app — it is not stored by Home Eats afterward. Anthropic processes this data under its own privacy policy.

Restaurant search

When you search for a restaurant, your search text and (if you've granted location access) your approximate location are sent through Home Eats' backend server to the Google Places API, which returns matching restaurants, ratings, hours, and photos. This data is used only to perform that search and is not stored by Home Eats afterward. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy.

What Home Eats does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in, or advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties for their own marketing purposes

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change, and the update will be reflected here before it takes effect.
